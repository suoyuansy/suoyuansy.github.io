## About Me

Hi there! I'm Suo Yuan, currently an undergraduate student majoring in Photogrammetry at the School of Remote Sensing and Information Engineering [(RSGIS)](https://rsgis.whu.edu.cn), Wuhan University [(WHU)](https://www.whu.edu.cn).

At present, I have been admitted to the Aerospace Information Research Institute [(AIR-CAS)](https://aircas.cas.cn), University of Chinese Academy of Sciences [(UCAS)](https://www.ucas.ac.cn) for a direct Ph.D. program.

During my undergraduate studies, I focused on photogrammetry principles and methods, computer vision and pattern recognition. I am self-taught in mathematical modeling approaches, C++ programming, and data structures and algorithms.

In my spare time, I led teams in robot competitions including [RoboMaster](https://www.robomaster.com), [RAICOM](https://www.raicom.com.cn), China Robotics and Artificial Intelligence Competition [(CRAIC)](https://www.caairobot.com), and China University Creative Robot Competition [(robotcontest)](http://www.robotcontest.cn). Through these competitions, I learned knowledge related to Ubuntu, ROS/ROS2, embedded development, electronics, mechanics, 3D modeling, and 3D printing.

Currently, I am working on my graduation project with [Prof. Kaichang Di](https://aircas.cas.cn/sourcedb/cn/expert/yjy/201811/t20181106_5165298.html) at the Planetary Mapping and Remote Sensing [(PMRS)](http://www.pmrslab.cn/index.html) Laboratory.
